# Artwork credits

All artwork on tranduc.dev is in the public domain. Each file here was cropped
and baked into a tritone (shadow `#0f0a1a`, mid `#3b1a7a`, highlight
`#e6ff00`; the lower half of each plate's tones is held at the page
background) with `scripts/prepare-art.mjs`.

## Design inspiration

The site's design language (display type, tinted classical engravings, film grain, parallax) was inspired by the Nous Research website. (Footer: "Design inspired by the Nous Research website".) No logos, images or other assets from that site are used.
